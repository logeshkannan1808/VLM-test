Sethu Institute of Technology

Sethu Institute of Technology (SIT) is a well-known engineering college located in
Pulloor, Kariapatti Taluk, Virudhunagar District, Tamil Nadu, India. The institute
was established with the vision of providing quality technical education and
developing skilled professionals who can contribute to society and industry.

The college is affiliated with Anna University, Chennai, and approved by the
All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). Sethu Institute of Technology
offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various branches of engineering
and technology.

Major undergraduate programs include Computer Science and Engineering,
Electronics and Communication Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering,
Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, and Information Technology.
Postgraduate programs are offered in selected specializations to enhance
advanced technical knowledge and research skills.

The campus is equipped with modern infrastructure, well-maintained laboratories,
a central library with digital resources, seminar halls, and smart classrooms.
The institution also focuses on holistic student development through technical
clubs, cultural activities, sports, workshops, and industry interaction programs.

Sethu Institute of Technology emphasizes discipline, ethics, innovation, and
industry-oriented learning. The college has a dedicated placement and training
cell that works towards improving students’ employability by organizing training
sessions, internships, and campus recruitment drives.

With experienced faculty members and a student-centered approach, Sethu Institute
of Technology strives to create a strong foundation for students to achieve
academic excellence and professional success.
